How it works

Each car is described by fourteen numbers: eight lengths that shape its chassis, plus the radius, density and mounting point of each of its two wheels. The first generation is random.

A car scores by how far right it travels, plus a bonus for its average speed. It dies when it stops making progress for ten seconds. Once every car has died, the best ones are paired up, their genes spliced together and lightly mutated, and the next generation sets off.

The terrain gets steeper the further you go, and is built from the track seed — the same seed always generates the same course, so runs can be compared and shared.

A modernised rebuild of rednuht's Genetic Cars, itself inspired by BoxCar2D. Physics by planck.js.
